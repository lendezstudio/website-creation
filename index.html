How It Works

A clear process, start to launch.

You don't need to understand web development to work with Lyneth Studio — just your own business.

01

Discover

Understand the business, customers, goals, current online presence, and what the website needs to accomplish.

02

Strategy

Plan the website structure, customer journey, content hierarchy, calls to action, and visual direction.

03

Design & Build

Create the responsive website with thoughtful design, content presentation, interaction, and functionality.

04

Refine

Review the website together and make agreed revisions.

05

Launch

Connect the domain, prepare the website for launch, and make it publicly accessible.
