About

Lyneth Lendez
Founder, Lyneth Studio

I started Lyneth Studio with a simple belief: a business's website should reflect the quality of the business behind it.

A lot of great businesses rely almost entirely on social media, marketplace listings, or outdated websites. Customers may like what they see, but they still have to search for information, scroll through posts, send multiple messages, or piece together what the business actually offers.

I create websites that bring that information together into one thoughtful, professional experience — whatever the industry.

Every project begins with understanding the business first. Then the design follows.
