Before You Ask

A few things worth knowing.

What kinds of businesses do you work with?

Businesses across different industries, including construction, restaurants, hospitality, financial and professional services, local businesses, and personal brands. If you are not sure the studio is a fit, describe the business and I will tell you.

Who will I actually be working with?

Lyneth, directly, from the first conversation through to launch. There is no account manager passing messages along.

Can I get social media management without a website?

Yes. Social media management is its own service line. It runs on its own or alongside a site, whichever the business needs.

What does the process look like?

Five steps: Discover, Strategy, Design and Build, Refine, Launch. You bring the knowledge of your business. You do not need to follow the technical side.

How much will it cost?

Every project is quoted to its scope, since a one-page launch and a full multi-page site are different jobs. Share a rough budget in your message and I will tell you early whether it lines up.
