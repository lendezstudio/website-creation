About

A studio of one, working closely with each client.

I'm Lyneth, the designer behind Lendez Studio.

Lendez Studio grew from my interest in helping businesses present themselves more professionally online. Too many good businesses — hotels, resorts, local shops, service providers — are represented online by a Facebook page that was never meant to be a website.

I believe a website should do more than look attractive. It should make it easier for people to understand a business, trust it, and take the next step — whether that's sending a message, checking availability, or booking a call.

I combine thoughtful design, clear messaging, and modern development to create websites that businesses can confidently share with their customers. Hospitality has become a particular focus, since I enjoy thinking about how a website can capture the feeling of a place while making it easy for guests to reach out directly.

Approach

What clients can expect.

01

Built Around Your Business

The website reflects the actual business — its tone, its customers, its goals — rather than forcing it into a generic template.

02

Strategy Before Decoration

Every design decision is there to support customer understanding, trust, and action — not just to look good.

03

Designed for Mobile

Many customers discover a business from their phone first. The mobile experience receives the same attention as desktop, not an afterthought.

04

Personal Collaboration

You work directly with me from the first conversation through launch — not passed between account managers and departments.

Philosophy

Good design gets attention. Good strategy gives it direction.

A website is often a customer's first real impression of a business — before a call, before a visit, before a booking. It should communicate clearly, build trust, and make the next step obvious.

That's the standard behind every project at Lendez Studio, whether it's a full hospitality website or a focused landing page.
