About

Lyneth Lendez
Founder

I run Lendez Studio as a studio of one. When you work with me, you are working directly with the person designing your website and running your social media, from the first conversation through launch.

A lot of good businesses rely almost entirely on social media, marketplace listings, or an outdated website. Customers may like what they see, but they still have to search for information, scroll through posts, send several messages, or piece together what the business actually offers.

Every project begins with understanding the business first. Then the design and the content follow.

From the job site to the digital space

I started my career as a civil engineer, working on-site and building things in an industry I genuinely enjoyed. Even then, I was curious about what was possible online, so I started learning new skills on the side, exploring different ways to build something of my own.

For a while, I balanced both worlds. Over time, though, what I wanted started to shift. I wanted more flexibility, more room for creativity, and the freedom to shape my own work and my own life.

That curiosity eventually led me to website creation and social media management. What began as something I was just learning quickly became something I loved: the mix of creativity, strategy, and problem-solving, and especially the satisfaction of helping businesses present themselves better online and build a presence that actually reflects who they are.

Today, I bring the same attention to detail, discipline, and problem-solving I built in engineering into the digital work I do now. I get to create, keep learning, help businesses grow their online presence, and do it all with the freedom to work from wherever life takes me.

It wasn't the career I originally planned. It's the one that ended up feeling right.
