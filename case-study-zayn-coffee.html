A digital home for Zayn Coffee’s story, menu, and experiences.

A brand-first website for a specialty coffee shop in Hilongos, Leyte, built to bring its story, menu, and offerings online in one place.

The Challenge

More than a product list.

Zayn Coffee PH wanted more than a website that simply displayed its products. The business needed a digital space where customers could discover the story behind the cafe, explore the menu conveniently, and learn about merchandise and additional offerings.

The Solution

A brand-focused website, built around the story.

Lendez Studio created a website that prioritizes Zayn Coffee’s identity and makes its offerings easier to explore. The site brings together the cafe’s origin story, from its start as “Do it Lattè” in 2023 to the physical space it is today, alongside a full digital menu, merchandise, and information on its mobile coffee event services.

  • A dedicated brand storytelling experience, including the cafe’s origin and mission
  • An easily accessible digital menu with full pricing
  • Merchandise presentation
  • Information on mobile coffee event services
  • Responsive layouts for different devices
  • A design reflecting the cafe’s warm, rustic tropical character
